In this policy, "Zignal Nexora" refers to Zignal Nexora S. A.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the zignalnexora.com website and our commercial relationship with clients and people who contact us. We distinguish two situations, because our role and your rights differ in each:

  • We act as a controller when we decide why and how data is processed — for example, the data you send us through the contact form on this site.
  • We act as a processor when we handle data on a client's behalf and on their instructions — for example, the messages handled by a WhatsApp bot or an omnichannel inbox we built for that client. In that case our client is the controller and their own privacy policy governs the relationship with end users (see section 8).

3. What data we process

3.1 Data you give us directly

Through the contact form on this site we collect only:

  • Full name (required)
  • Work email address (required)
  • Company (required)
  • Phone number, including country code (optional)
  • Product of interest, selected from a list
  • Free-text message describing the process you want to automate (optional)

Please do not include sensitive data or confidential third-party information in the message field. For that, we prefer a channel agreed with you.

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • Technical server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its DNS is managed through Cloudflare. These providers automatically record data such as IP address, browser type, request date and time, and the page requested, for content delivery, security and abuse prevention.
  • Local storage in your browser. We store two of your preferences using localStorage: the visual theme (zn-theme) and the language (zn-lang). These are not cookies, are never sent to any server, do not identify you, and you can clear them from your browser at any time.

This site uses no tracking cookies, no analytics and no advertising pixels — including the Meta pixel. We do not build profiles and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects concerning you.

3.3 Data reaching third parties

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When this happens, your browser connects to Google's servers and discloses your IP address and basic request data to them. We have no control over that processing, which is governed by Google's own policies.

4. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request, and your consent when submitting the form.
  • To maintain the commercial relationship and follow up on contracted projects. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To operate and protect the site against abuse, spam and attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and secure.
  • To comply with legal, accounting and tax obligations that apply to us. Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes, and we do not use it to send you marketing unrelated to your enquiry.

5. Who we share it with

We only share data with providers whose services we need in order to operate, under confidentiality obligations and processing data solely according to our instructions:

  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — DNS management and domain protection.
  • Formspree, Inc. — contact form processing. When you submit the form, the data you entered is transmitted to Formspree, which stores it and forwards it to us by email.
  • Hostinger — corporate email service.
  • Google LLC — web font delivery (Google Fonts).

We may also disclose information where there is a legal obligation, a request from a competent authority, or to defend our rights. If we add any other service that processes personal data in the future, we will update this list.

6. International transfers

Some of the providers listed above are located outside Ecuador, mainly in the United States and the European Union. Where this happens, we seek to ensure appropriate safeguards — such as contractual data protection clauses — in line with Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP).

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a commercial relationship: up to 24 months from the last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client data: for the duration of the contractual relationship and, afterwards, for the applicable legal, accounting and tax limitation periods.
  • Technical logs: according to each infrastructure provider's retention periods.

Once those periods expire, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

8. Data we process on behalf of our clients

Our services — process automation, metrics dashboards, WhatsApp bots, omnichannel platform and on-premises analytics — involve processing personal data that belongs to our clients and relates to their own end users. In those cases:

  • We act as a processor, following the client's documented instructions.
  • We do not use that data for our own purposes, we do not sell it, and we do not disclose it to unauthorised third parties.
  • We apply confidentiality and the security measures contractually agreed with the client.
  • In on-premises deployments, the data stays within the client's own infrastructure and is not transferred to our systems.
  • When the service ends, we return or delete the data as agreed with the client.

If you are an end user of a company that works with us and you want to exercise your rights, you should contact that company, which is the controller. If you write to us instead, we will forward your request to the relevant client and let you know.

9. WhatsApp and Meta platforms

When we implement solutions on the WhatsApp Business Platform or other Meta platforms for a client, processing is limited to what is necessary to operate that support channel on the client's behalf. This may include the end user's phone number, profile name and the content of the messages they exchange with the business.

  • That processing is carried out on the client's instructions; the client is the controller towards the end user.
  • We use that data solely to provide the contracted messaging and support service.
  • We do not use data obtained from Meta platforms for advertising purposes, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to data brokers, and we do not use it to build profiles unrelated to the purpose of the service.
  • We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and the applicable WhatsApp Business policies.
  • Anyone may request deletion of their data through the route described in section 11.

10. Your rights

Under Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection, as a data subject you may exercise the following rights free of charge:

  • Access — find out what data of yours we process and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification and updating — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your data when it is no longer necessary or you withdraw consent.
  • Objection — object to our processing in certain circumstances.
  • Suspension of processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Not to be subject to automated decisions that significantly affect you.

To exercise them, write to privacy@zignalnexora.com. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on the request. We will respond within the periods set by the LOPDP and, in practice, we aim to do so within 15 business days.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with Ecuador's Superintendency for Personal Data Protection (Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales).

11. How to request deletion of your data

Data deletion request

Send an email to privacy@zignalnexora.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include:

  • The name and email address you used to contact us.
  • If applicable, the phone number or channel (for example, WhatsApp) you used.
  • A short description of what you want deleted.

We will confirm receipt and tell you the outcome. We will delete the data unless we are required to keep it under a legal obligation, in which case we will explain which one and for how long. If the data was processed on behalf of one of our clients, we will forward your request to that controller and let you know.

12. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data: encryption in transit via HTTPS across the whole site, role-based access control following the principle of least privilege, environment segregation, and credential management using secrets. No system is entirely infallible; if a breach affecting your data occurred and posed a risk, we would notify it as required by the applicable rules.

13. Minors

Our services are aimed at businesses and at adults. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we find that we have received a minor's data without the appropriate authorisation, we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or in applicable law. The version in force will always be published at this same address, with its update date. If a change is substantial, we will communicate it through an appropriate channel.
